## 3. The "Value Stack" Implementation Method
To maximize your Average Order Value (AOV) and virality, never sell a single file. Sell a **System**.

**Example: "The MedSpa Growth System"**
1. **Core Product (The Strategy):** PDF Guide on getting 10 new clients/mo.
2. **Support Asset 1 (The Speed):** 50+ AI Prompts for high-converting Instagram ads.
3. **Support Asset 2 (The System):** Notion Template to track leads and appointments.
4. **Support Asset 3 (The Visual):** 20 Canva Templates for "Before & After" posts.
5. **The Bonus (The Friction-Killer):** A "What to Post" daily cheat sheet.

## 4. Roadmap to Launch
